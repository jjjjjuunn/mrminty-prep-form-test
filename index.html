Packing rules

  • Ship in a box.
  • Cards in a semi-rigid holder between two pieces of cardboard, then bubble wrap.
  • Signature service required.
  • Overnight or 2nd day shipping for anything of significant value.
  • No "Mr. Minty", "cards" or hobby-related names on the shipping label or packaging.
  • Avoid shipping on weekends.
